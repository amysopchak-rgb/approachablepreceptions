    <h3> <p> My Art and Teaching </p> </h3><br>
    <p> I enjoy working in watercolour, oils, acrylics, and water-mixable oils. For me, painting is about much more than the materials or the finished image. Art tells a stories. It captures experiences, emotions, memories, and important moments in our lives. <br>
      Through teaching, I want to help people feel comfortable expressing themselves through art and discovering their own stories. I believe that art can help us recognise the common ground we share and connect with one another. <br>
     Painting is a place of connection--with spirit, life, and people. <br>
     In addition to painting, I enjoy exploring printmaking, lithography, zinc-plate etching, and block painting. Each medium offers a different way to explore ideas, tell stories, and connect with the world around us. <br>
